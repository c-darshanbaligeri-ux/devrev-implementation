## New Endpoints

### Vistas

* [`/vistas.delete`](../api-reference/vistas.md#delete): Deletes a vista
* [`/vistas.get`](../api-reference/vistas.md#get-post): Gets a vista's information
* [`/vistas.list`](../api-reference/vistas.md#list-post): Lists available vistas

### Vista Group Items

* [`/vistas.groups.delete`](../api-reference/vistas.md#groups-delete): Deletes a vista group item
* [`/vistas.groups.get`](../api-reference/vistas.md#groups-get-post): Gets a vista group item's information
* [`/vistas.groups.list`](../api-reference/vistas.md#groups-list-post): Lists the available vista group items
